IT Governance and Risk Management Report: 

Global E-Commerce Company

A hypothetical global e-commerce company faces numerous risks, with data breaches representing a critical threat given the sensitive customer information it handles. At the same time, robust data security presents a strategic opportunity to strengthen customer trust and gain a competitive advantage.

Governance and Risk Mitigation Framework

The company will adopt COBIT 2019 as its IT governance framework to ensure alignment between IT initiatives and business objectives and to establish clear policies, roles, and performance metrics (ISACA, 2018). The use of COBIT will enable a systematic approach to risk management, prioritising high-impact threats such as data breaches. In addition, the company will implement an ITIL-based disaster recovery (DR) strategy that includes regular data backups, multi-region redundancy, and tested incident response plans to minimise downtime and data loss.

Compliance and Ethical Considerations

The organisation must comply with international regulations, including the GDPR and PCI DSS, to ensure lawful and ethical IT management. This requires transparent data handling, obtaining customer consent, and conducting regular audits. IT professionals also have an ethical obligation to uphold integrity and confidentiality, refrain from misusing customer data, and proactively address vulnerabilities (Floridi & Taddeo, 2016).

Conclusion

By integrating COBIT with ITIL-based disaster recovery planning and robust compliance measures, the company not only mitigates risks but also reaffirms its ethical commitment and professional responsibility, thereby strengthening stakeholder confidence.
