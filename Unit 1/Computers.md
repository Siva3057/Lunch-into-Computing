What is Computing and Introduction to Python?
Computing is the process of using computer technology, both hardware and software, to complete goal-orientated tasks, manage and process data, and solve problems. It encompasses everything from the design and building of systems to the algorithmic processes that drive them.
Core Disciplines of Computing
1.	Computer Science (CS): The theoretical and practical study of algorithms, data structures, computation, and information processing.
2.	Software Engineering (SE): The systematic application of engineering principles to the design, development, testing, and maintenance of software systems.
3.	Computer Engineering (CE): The integration of electronic engineering and computer science to design and build computer hardware, microprocessors, and embedded systems.
4.	Information Technology (IT): The practical application of computing technology to support organisational business operations, network infrastructure, and system administration.
5.	Information Systems (IS): The study of how people, technology, and organisational processes interact to gather, store, filter, and distribute data.
